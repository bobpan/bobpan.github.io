About Us

DataPick AI was born from a simple belief: data should be accessible, not locked behind technical barriers. In an era of information overload, extracting valuable, structured data from web pages often requires programming knowledge or complex tools. We aim to change that.

Our Vision

We envision a world where anyone, regardless of their technical background, can easily and efficiently extract the data they need from any web page. DataPick AI is more than just a tool; it's an enabler, helping users focus on insights and value creation, rather than getting bogged down in the tedious process of data collection.

Our Mission

By combining cutting-edge AI technology with an intuitive user interface, DataPick AI is committed to providing a seamless data extraction experience. We promise to:

  • **Simplify Complexity:** Transform the complex process of web parsing and data structuring into a simple natural language conversation.
  • **Enhance Efficiency:** Drastically reduce data acquisition time from hours or even days to mere seconds.
  • **Ensure Security:** Strictly adhere to privacy protection principles, ensuring user data is secure and its handling transparent during the extraction process.
  • **Innovate Continuously:** Constantly explore and integrate the latest AI technologies to provide users with more powerful and intelligent services.

Our Team (Bob Pan)

DataPick AI was founded and is led by independent developer Bob Pan. Bob is a seasoned product and technology expert with many years in the AI field. He is a firm believer that AI should serve to enhance everyone's productivity. DataPick AI is his practice of bringing advanced AI technology to everyday users.
